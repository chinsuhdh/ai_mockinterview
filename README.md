## 🎯 Core Concept
**AI Mock Interviewer** is a web-based application designed to help final-year students and fresh graduates practice job interviews confidently. By simulating real recruiters using AI voice and chat capabilities, users can improve their answers, refine their communication skills, and perform better in actual interviews without paying for expensive soft-skill courses.

---

## 🛑 The Problem
* **High Anxiety:** 75% of final-year students feel anxious before job interviews (NACE 2024).
* **Lack of Practice Partners:** Students struggle to find peers for English or specialized technical interviews.
* **Expensive Alternatives:** Traditional soft-skill courses cost around 1–2 million VND per session and provide generic advice.
* **No Personalization:** Existing feedback is rarely tailored to specific Job Descriptions (JDs).
* **Validation:** In a local survey of 20 university students graduating in 2026, 80% admitted they “don’t know how to answer tough interview questions.”

---

## ✨ Solution Concept
Our system uses AI to act as a recruiter, conducting highly context-aware interviews based on user-provided Job Descriptions.

**The 3-Step User Flow:**
1. **Input JD:** User uploads or pastes a specific Job Description.
2. **Mock Interview:** The AI recruiter conducts the interview in real-time via voice or chat.
3. **Instant Evaluation:** The system scores the answers, highlights weaknesses, and provides actionable suggestions.

**Magic Moment:** Users receive immediate, personalized feedback on both their speaking delivery and content accuracy.

* **Backend:** .NET

---

## 📊 Target Customer & Market Timing
* **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** Final-year university students and fresh graduates preparing for internships or full-time roles.
* **Early Adopters:** IT, Business, and Communication majors facing technical or English interviews.
* **Market Timing:** The post-COVID era has normalized online interviews. AI voice/chat APIs are now highly capable and affordable, aligning perfectly with universities' increasing emphasis on career readiness.

---

## 💰 Revenue Model
We operate on a **Freemium** model:
* **Free Tier:** 3 mock interviews per month.
* **Premium Tier:** 99,000 VND/month for unlimited interviews and detailed analytics.
* **Unit Economics Goal:** Maintain a CAC of ~15,000 VND via online ads against an estimated 3-month LTV of ~250,000 VND.
